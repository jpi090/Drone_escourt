SRS COMPLIANCE & ETHICS

Built on Binding Regulatory Constraints

Safety is enforced by the server state machine, not client-side checkboxes.

🛡️

Server-Authoritative Flight Gate

The server refuses launch requests if battery is under minimum or airframe maintenance is due. The client cannot coerce the drone into an unsafe state.

⚡

Hard 22% Reserve Return

When battery discharge reaches 22%, the system automatically overrides mission commands and initiates Return-to-Dock to eliminate crash-landings.

👁️

Zero Autonomous Person Classification

In strict compliance with RC-5 and privacy regulations, the platform never automatically classifies persons or applies facial recognition. Bystanders are blurred.

📜

Append-Only Audit Log

Every alert, operator acknowledgment, escalation, and flight abort is permanently recorded in an append-only log with precise timestamps and actor attribution.
